== Unreleased

* 1 major enhancement:
  * Rules whose recursion appears only in tail position (direct and mutual
    right recursion) are now compiled with an explicit execution stack, so
    deeply recursive inputs no longer exhaust the Ruby call stack. Recursive
    rules embedding Ruby semantic predicate blocks or using `super`, and
    recursion outside tail position, cannot use the explicit stack; the
    compiler reports why and falls back to plain recursion.
